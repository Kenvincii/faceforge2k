FACEFORGE 2K / PROTOTYPE 0.2
PS5-first • local photo preview • no game connection

Build the face.
Own the details.

Turn a reference into a structured recreation plan for the NBA 2K face editor. This prototype helps organize visual observations and manual choices; it does not yet infer a face from pixels or connect to your console.

01 / Reference & setup

For best comparison, use a clear, front-facing image with even lighting. Only use images you have permission to use.

02 / Shape notes

Move the sliders to record your visual estimate. They are relative notes, not official NBA 2K slider values.

03 / Recreation plan

Use this as a checklist while working inside the game's own face editor. Match broad proportions first, then refine features and facial hair.

Compatibility note: this does not upload a custom player face to PS5. NBA 2K's official face scan is designed for your own face; arbitrary photo-to-player imports are not a documented PS5 workflow.
Your recreation plan will appear here. Start by selecting a reference photo, recording the shape notes, then choose “Generate recreation plan.”
Privacy: the photo is previewed locally in your browser. This page does not upload it. No face-recognition model or automatic 3D reconstruction is running in this version.
